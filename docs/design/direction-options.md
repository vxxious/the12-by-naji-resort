# Impeccable Direction Options

These are direction comps, not final page screenshots. They use the resort's public logo and property photographs to test composition, hierarchy, density, and booking treatment. Final copy remains subject to the content confirmations in the audit.

## Direction A — Modern Editorial Luxury

- **Concept:** A contemporary hospitality journal where the room photography and one oversized editorial statement share the page as a single composition.
- **Palette:** parchment `#F5F0E7`, ink `#1C1915`, white `#FFFFFF`, brand gold `#C89A3D`, dark ground `#12110F`.
- **Type:** Libre Caslon Display for editorial headings; Manrope for body, navigation, and form UI.
- **Homepage:** immersive room-led opening; Apartment Resort introduction; featured rooms as large editorial spreads; amenities as captioned photography; curated gallery; tours/events preview; booking close.
- **Navigation:** quiet ivory masthead, compact wordmark, widely spaced links, rectangular gold booking action.
- **Hero:** one dominant interior photograph with an ivory editorial band crossing it; stay planner anchored across the lower edge.
- **Rooms:** full-width image-and-copy spreads alternating rhythm, with room facts and rates aligned to fine rules rather than cards.
- **Animation:** subtle image-mask reveals, headline line reveals, and restrained crossfades; 180–600ms; no continuous parallax.
- **Mobile:** photo first, then the editorial statement; stay planner becomes an accessible stacked sheet; room spreads become a single reading column.
- **Why it fits:** It elevates the existing photography and Apartment Resort story without introducing an unrelated luxury vocabulary.
- **Risk:** High-contrast editorial type can feel like a magazine rather than a place to book if the planner and room facts are not kept prominent.

## Direction B — Minimal Architectural Luxury

- **Concept:** The resort as a measured architectural composition: building, rooms, gardens, and activities organized with quiet precision.
- **Palette:** chalk `#F2F1ED`, carbon `#121212`, concrete `#A8A39A`, white `#FFFFFF`, brass `#B78B3E`, dark surface `#1A1A19`.
- **Type:** Manrope as the primary variable grotesk; Azeret Mono for small location, rate, and category annotations.
- **Homepage:** aerial architectural opening; two-block introduction; apartment types; amenity plan; gallery; tours/events; booking close and contact.
- **Navigation:** one exact horizontal line with a small mark, concise labels, and a single brass action.
- **Hero:** the aerial property photograph becomes a large architectural plate across a twelve-column grid; the stay planner completes the grid at the fold.
- **Rooms:** image plates, measured specifications, and clear apartment grouping; dense facts remain readable without turning into cards.
- **Animation:** line/plate reveals, 2–4% controlled image drift, clear booking state transitions, and no ornamental motion.
- **Mobile:** grid collapses to four columns; typography scales fluidly; annotations move below images; the planner becomes a compact vertical sequence.
- **Why it fits:** The strongest unique evidence is the physical compound, two blocks, real rooms, and amenities. This system turns that evidence into the identity while making a large room catalogue easy to navigate.
- **Risk:** It can feel severe unless warm room photography, hospitable copy, and the brand gold are used with care.
- **Impeccable raises:** one decisive accent instead of scattered gold; every booking state is explicit; the grid adapts rather than acting as decoration; material contrast is carried across the whole page.
- **Preview:** `.impeccable/mocks/decision/direction-b-minimal-architectural.png`

## Direction C — Cinematic Hospitality

- **Concept:** A restrained film opening that lets the real property lead, then moves through rooms and experiences as carefully paced scenes.
- **Palette:** absolute black `#090909`, soft bone `#ECE7DC`, muted brass `#C79A45`, smoke `#272727`, light surface `#F4F1EA`.
- **Type:** Italiana for title-scale moments; Manrope for navigation, captions, and booking controls.
- **Homepage:** immersive exterior opening; Apartment Resort film sequence; featured rooms; amenities as visual chapters; tours as actual video; events; booking finale.
- **Navigation:** transparent over the opening image, becoming a solid high-contrast bar after scroll; a clear gold booking action remains visible.
- **Hero:** full-bleed exterior footage frame with an integrated dark booking rail and a small Utako/Abuja location cue.
- **Rooms:** large horizontal stills with minimal captions, progressive reveal, and direct room-specific booking actions.
- **Animation:** one orchestrated GSAP/ScrollTrigger story, slow clip reveals, controlled 3–5% parallax, and cinematic page transitions; complete reduced-motion still version.
- **Mobile:** full-height opening with a compact title; booking opens as a focused bottom sheet; scroll scenes become stacked stills rather than scrubbed sequences.
- **Why it fits:** The site already has drone photography and four resort-tour videos, so a cinematic direction can use real media instead of decorative effects.
- **Risk:** It has the highest image-quality, implementation, and performance demands and could overstate the property if the media is graded too aggressively.
- **Preview:** `.impeccable/mocks/decision/direction-c-cinematic-hospitality.png`

## Direction D — Contemporary Boutique Resort

- **Concept:** A warm independent-hotel guest folio that makes the apartment experience feel personal, relaxed, and considered.
- **Palette:** warm ivory `#F4EFE6`, charcoal `#24211D`, brand gold `#B88A3B`, walnut `#6B4D37`, linen `#FBF8F2`, dark ground `#161411`.
- **Type:** Gloock for characterful display headings; Onest for approachable, precise body and form text.
- **Homepage:** residential interior opening; Elysian/Oasis introduction; editorial room selection; amenities and gardens; gallery; tours/events; booking and contact.
- **Navigation:** warm ivory bar, compact brand mark, understated active underline, rectangular gold booking action.
- **Hero:** off-centre living-room photograph crossing the fold, exact Apartment Resort copy, and a quiet integrated stay-planning row.
- **Rooms:** Elysian and Oasis presented as two visual chapters, then individual rooms as large image-led rows with concise facts.
- **Animation:** gentle image crossfades, text settles, refined underline/arrow motion, and simple gallery transitions; no scroll hijacking.
- **Mobile:** image-led single column with booking kept near the top; Elysian/Oasis become thumb-friendly chapter tabs; room facts remain visible without accordions.
- **Why it fits:** It connects directly to the current warm black/gold identity and the residential side of the Apartment Resort proposition.
- **Risk:** Warm ivory plus serif is familiar in boutique hospitality; execution must rely on the real folio composition and room taxonomy to avoid becoming generic.
- **Preview:** `.impeccable/mocks/decision/direction-d-contemporary-boutique.png`

## Recommendation

**Direction B — Minimal Architectural Luxury** is the strongest foundation. It uses the property's most defensible evidence—the buildings, apartment blocks, rooms, and amenities—while making the seventeen-name catalogue manageable. It also absorbs the black/gold identity without slipping into generic “luxury gold” styling. Direction D is the strongest warmer alternative if welcome and softness should outweigh architectural distinction.
