# Selected direction: B — Minimal Architectural Luxury

Selected on 24 October 2026 through the Impeccable decision flow.

Finish review: **ship** on 24 October 2026. The implemented system is recorded in `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json`.

## Surface brief

- Treat the property as a measured architectural composition rather than a generic hotel template.
- Use the approved decision comp at `.impeccable/mocks/decision/direction-b-minimal-architectural.png` as the compositional reference.
- Build on a chalk, carbon, concrete, white, and restrained brass palette.
- Use Manrope for the principal interface and Azeret Mono for location, room, rate, and state annotations.
- Use a twelve-column desktop grid and a four-column mobile reduction.
- Let real property photography carry the visual identity through large architectural plates.
- Keep navigation quiet and exact, with one decisive brass booking action.
- Present Elysian and Oasis as clear collections, then expose room facts without card-heavy UI.
- Use line reveals, subtle image drift, and explicit state transitions; avoid ornamental motion.
- Preserve warmth through room photography, plain hospitality language, and generous whitespace.
- Support light and dark themes with semantic tokens and equivalent contrast.
- Treat the booking experience as a request/integration boundary until MyRoomStatus and Paystack are verified end to end.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The property itself is the identity: a measured composition of apartments, shared amenities and grounds. It refuses the category-default full-bleed hotel hero followed by a row of generic room cards.

**OWN-WORLD:** Chalk and carbon grounds, precise hairline rules, large photographic plates, restrained brass actions and compact mono annotations create the system. Rectangular controls and a twelve-column grid replace soft cards and ornamental luxury cues.

**STORY:** A visitor understands that this is an apartment resort in Utako, sees the real compound and apartment catalogue, then moves into a clearly qualified stay request without being promised unverified availability.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A quiet horizontal masthead sits above an oversized two-line proposition. A large property plate occupies the lower field, with location annotation over the image and the stay planner completing the grid at its base. The brass booking action is the only dominant accent.

**FORM:** Minimal Architectural Luxury, first in the ordered direction list. Seed reference: `seed:1b3fcc241a4b8705`.
